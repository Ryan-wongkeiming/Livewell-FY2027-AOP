Why we believe this growth is real

Four data points that confirm FY2026 performance is structural — and sustainable into FY2027.

Consistent account acquisition
46 first-time buyers onboarded at FY2026 launch (Jul 2025), with steady 4–16 new first-time buyers every month since. Total: 140 accounts across 10 months. Acquisition is not front-loaded or seasonal — the pipeline has been active every single month.
March 55.7B VND was broad-based
Breakdown: 40.2B from accounts buying since before FY2026 (72%), 9.0B from accounts started 3–6 months prior (16%), 6.5B from accounts started within last 3 months (12%). Not one large order — revenue accelerated across all customer tiers simultaneously.
First-time buyers outperform existing
Accounts that joined in FY2026 average 119M VND/month each — 22% higher than accounts that were already buying before FY2026 (97M VND/month each). Every new account added is contributing more revenue than the average existing account.
Contract target exceeded by +34%
The agreed annual target between Livewell and a2 Milk was 303B VND for FY2026. Actual revenue to date is 326B — already above target with three months (Apr, May, Jun) remaining. Full-year projection: approximately 426B VND, or 40% above the original commitment.

Bottom line: FY2026 is not a peak — it is the new floor
Monthly revenue grew from 15.5B in July 2025 to 55.7B in March 2026 — a 3.6x increase within a single fiscal year. 140 new accounts now generate 35.5% of monthly revenue at a higher per-account rate than the established base. The contract target of 303B was exceeded three months early.

What this means for FY2027: The 426B FY2026 forecast is built on broad, diversified growth — not a single account or product push. All three FY2027 scenarios — from +1.9% to +67.1% year-over-year growth — are anchored to this confirmed base. Key definitions

FY2026
Fiscal Year 2026: July 2025 – June 2026. Three months remain (Apr, May, Jun 2026).
FY2027
Fiscal Year 2027: July 2026 – June 2027. The forecast target year — has not started yet.
B / M
Billion / Million Vietnamese Dong (VND). All figures on this page are in VND.
426B forecast
Projected full-year FY2026 revenue = actual invoices to date + forecast for remaining months. Updates daily as new invoices post.
Contract target (303B)
The annual revenue commitment agreed between Livewell Distribution and a2 Milk for FY2026.
First-time buyer
A customer with no purchase history at Livewell before July 2025. Their first-ever order was placed during FY2026. Not a returning dormant account.
Active account
Any direct customer (KH/KR code) that placed at least one a2 Milk order during FY2026. Total: 451.
35.5% revenue share
The share of monthly revenue generated by first-time FY2026 buyers. Measured on the most recent 3-month window, not a single month.
Current pace (434B)
What FY2027 revenue would be if the current monthly rate continues for 12 months with no further growth or decline.
Challenge I / II / III
Three progressively ambitious FY2027 targets. Growth is volume-driven (sell more units at current pricing). Price adjustments can be layered separately in the live model.
Year-over-Year (%)
FY2027 target divided by FY2026 forecast, minus 1. Example: 510B ÷ 426B − 1 = +19.7%.
"Live" values
Numbers that update automatically as new data loads. The FY2026 forecast, year-to-date actual, and all FY2027 targets recalculate daily. Values shown here reflect the model as of 27 April 2026.
