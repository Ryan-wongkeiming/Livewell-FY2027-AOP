Four pillars of evidence

Data-driven analysis proving the FY27 growth base is structurally sustainable — not a one-off spike.

Account acquisition curve
46 accounts onboarded at FY26 start (Jul 2025), with steady 4-16 per month since. Acquisition is consistent, not front-loaded. The pipeline is active and producing new revenue every month.
March spike decomposition
55.65B breakdown: 40.2B from established accounts accelerating (72%), 9.0B from recent starters (16%), 6.5B from new starters (12%). Broad-based growth across the customer base — not one large order.
WRR sustainability confirmed
New FY26 accounts average 119M/month per account — 22% MORE productive than established accounts (97M/month). The WRR at 37.3B reflects real capacity expansion, not temporary inflation.
MAPE Signal 3 — explained
30% forecast variance was a POSITIVE surprise. Actual beat forecast by 49%. The model self-corrected — WRR absorbed March into its 3M window. April MAPE will normalise to Band 1-2 as the elevated pace becomes the new baseline.
